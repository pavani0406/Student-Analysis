Smart Campus Analytics: Predict, Optimize & Improve Student Success
1. Background
Colleges generate large amounts of data through attendance, internal assessments, examinations, LMS activity, placement participation, extracurricular activities, and student feedback. However, this data is often stored in disconnected systems and is rarely used proactively.

2. Challenge Statement
Build an Analytics & AI-powered Student Success Platform that can identify students who may require intervention and help college administrators take data-driven actions.

Move beyond simply building a dashboard. Use analytics and AI to convert student data into actionable insights and decision intelligence.

3. Core Scope
Data Integration
Bring student data from multiple sources into one unified student view, including:

Academic
Attendance
LMS
Engagement
Placement
Skills
Feedback
Student Success Score
Create a Student Success Score using relevant academic and engagement indicators.

At-Risk Identification
Identify students who may be at risk of:

Poor academic performance
Poor placement outcomes
Interactive Dashboard
Build an interactive dashboard for faculty and administrators showing:

Student Success Scores
Risk flags
Key trends
Relevant student insights
Suggested Data
Data Category	Examples
Academic	CGPA, marks, backlogs, subject performance
Attendance	Overall %, subject-wise attendance
LMS	Login frequency, assignment completion
Engagement	Events, clubs, hackathons, certifications
Placement	Aptitude score, coding score, mock interviews
Skills	Technical and soft-skill assessments
Feedback	Student satisfaction, faculty feedback
4. Bonus Features
Student Segmentation
Group students into meaningful segments, for example:

High academic performance but low placement readiness
Other segments that can support targeted interventions
Explainable Score
Show which indicators contribute most to each student's:

Success Score
Risk flag
5. Deliverables
Working dashboard prototype built on the integrated student data.
Short note explaining the Student Success Score, including the indicators used and how they are combined.
Short demo or a few slides showing the approach and key insights.
6. Evaluation Criteria — 100 Points
Criterion	Weight	What Judges Look For
Data Integration & Analysis	30%	Quality of integration across sources, data cleaning, and insights drawn from the combined data
Success Score & Risk Identification	25%	Relevant indicators, sensible scoring logic, and clear identification of at-risk students
Dashboard & Visualization	25%	Interactive, clear, and useful for faculty and administrators
Problem Understanding	10%	Understanding of the campus context and the needs of faculty and administrators
Presentation & Demo	10%	Working prototype demonstrated clearly through a short demo or a few slides
7. Bonus Credit
Bonus credit may be earned for:

Student Segmentation: Meaningful student groups that support targeted action.
Explainable Score: Visible drivers behind each Success Score and risk flag.
Bonus credit is added within the relevant evaluation criteria:

Segmentation → Success Score & Risk Identification
Explainable Score → Dashboard & Visualization
Bonus credit is capped at approximately 5–10 points in total.
